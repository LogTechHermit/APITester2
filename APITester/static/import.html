CSV format

Use a UTF-8 CSV with this exact header row. Actions are case-insensitive.

Action,Customer Number,Customer Name,Customer Type,Customer Category
  • Create: all four customer fields are required. Existing numbers are reported as errors.
  • Update: customer number and all three customer details are required; the customer must exist.
  • Delete: only customer number is required; the other fields may be blank.

Rows are applied independently. Successful rows stay saved even if other rows have errors. Maximum file size: 10 MB.
